The Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) under the Faculty of Electrical & Computer Engineering at **Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)** certifies that the B.Sc. Eng. degree in ETE comprises eight semesters within four academic years.

The certificate further confirms that the ETE curriculum covers theory and laboratory courses in ICT/ICE fields and that graduates are proficient in diverse ICT/ICE-related areas. Accordingly, the **B.Sc. Eng. degree in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering is considered equivalent to a B.Sc. in ICT/ICE**.
