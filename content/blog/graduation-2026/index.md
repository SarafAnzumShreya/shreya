## Finally Done and Dusted

After all the hardships and sleepless nights, it is finally over. I have reached the target I set for myself when I first came to RUET: graduation.

There were times when I felt that I was not working hard enough, that I was always falling short. Sometimes everything became so tiring that I wanted to give up, and I almost did. Then, out of nowhere, a sudden surge of motivation would carry me forward, and I would find a way to keep going.

In the end, it was not something extraordinary, but it was not insignificant either. I am satisfied with what I have achieved. I was the real "farmer murgi," and I still am, but one thing I have learned is that I can do something if I truly want to.

I still have a long way to go. I am not ready to confine myself within a boundary. There is so much more to see and so much more to explore.

I love life. The only thing I dislike is time. The thought of not being able to do or achieve everything I want in my short life will always haunt me. So I need to keep pushing beyond my boundaries.

Most of all, I am proud of myself and of the person I have become.
