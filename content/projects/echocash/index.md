A real-time currency recognition system designed to help visually impaired individuals identify banknotes independently. EchoCash uses a YOLOv8-nano model with a Squeeze-and-Excitation-enhanced detection head to recognize USD, EUR, and BDT, then provides immediate voice feedback through a smartphone camera.

{{< figure src="example.jpg">}}

<!--more-->

The project combines deep learning and assistive technology to make everyday transactions more accessible, even when banknotes are worn, crumpled, or difficult to distinguish by touch.
