Welcome to RUET is an interactive campus-themed web game designed to introduce visitors to the university in a fun and engaging way. The game presents a guided journey through the university environment, where players can explore campus highlights, complete challenges, and learn more about RUET through an interactive experience.

<!--more-->

## How it works

The game starts with a simple and welcoming interface, where users can begin exploring the university experience. Players can move through different sections, complete tasks, and unlock progression as they learn more about the campus. The experience is designed to feel light, interactive, and educational rather than a traditional static landing page.

A major feature of the project is the course system. Users can choose from up to 15 courses and complete learning paths within the game experience, making it more engaging and game-like. At the end of the experience, players can download a certificate as a reward for completing the journey.

## Key features

- interactive campus-themed gameplay
- educational exploration experience
- up to 15 course selections
- progress-based learning flow
- certificate download at the end
- simple yet engaging UI built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

## Game experience

The app includes a visually designed game surface and certificate page that make the experience feel complete and rewarding. Users can progress through the interface, interact with the flow, and finish with a downloadable certificate to mark their completion.
