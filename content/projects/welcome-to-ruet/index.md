Welcome to RUET is an interactive campus-themed web game. 

<!--more-->

## How it works

The game starts with a simple and welcoming interface. Players can collect the books relevant to their department in order to complete their degree. The experience is designed to feel light, interactive, and educational rather than a traditional static landing page.

A major feature of the game is the course system. Users can choose from up to 15 courses, making it more engaging and game-like. At the end of the experience, players can download a certificate as a reward for completing the journey.

## Key features

- interactive campus-themed gameplay
- up to 15 course selections
- progress-based learning flow
- certificate download at the end
- simple yet engaging UI built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

## Game experience

The app includes a visually designed game surface and certificate page that make the experience feel complete and rewarding. Users can progress through the interface, interact with the flow and finish with a downloadable certificate to mark their completion.
